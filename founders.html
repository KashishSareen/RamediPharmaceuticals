Meet Our Founders

The visionary leadership shaping Ramedi Pharma with purpose, professionalism, and an uncompromising commitment to healthcare trust.

Founder & Director

Suraj Shukla

Suraj Shukla

Founder & Director | Ramedi Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd.

My journey in the pharmaceutical industry began with a strong belief that healthcare is built on trust.

With a Bachelor's degree in Commerce (Account Honours) from Asansol, West Bengal, and 10 years of professional experience with Intas Pharmaceuticals in the CNS segment, I had the opportunity to understand the pharmaceutical industry closely and build lasting relationships with healthcare professionals.

This experience inspired me to take the entrepreneurial journey and establish Ramedi Pharmaceuticals with a simple vision - to build a professional Indian pharmaceutical company committed to quality, trust and responsible healthcare.

As we grow, our goal is not just to build brands, but to build lasting trust with doctors, patients and healthcare partners across India.

“India Comes for Trust.”

— Suraj Shukla | Founder & Director

Director

Priya Suraj Shukla

Priya Suraj Shukla

Director | Ramedi Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd.

Priya Suraj Shukla is a Director at Ramedi Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd., contributing to the company's day-to-day operations, coordination and business management.

She completed her graduation in Arts, with her education spanning Mumbai and Uttar Pradesh. Her professional experience includes working across marketing operations, logistics, coordination and business support, giving her a practical understanding of the operational side of an organization.

At Ramedi Pharmaceuticals, Priya plays an important role in supporting operational efficiency, coordination and smooth execution of business activities. Her structured approach and attention to detail complement the company's entrepreneurial and growth-focused vision.

Together with the leadership team, she is committed to building Ramedi Pharmaceuticals as a professional, trusted and growth-oriented pharmaceutical company.

“Building Trust. Growing Together.”

— Priya Suraj Shukla | Director
