2023

The Beginning

A clear vision for better healthcare

Ramedi Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2023 with a clear vision - to build a trusted Indian pharmaceutical company focused on quality, innovation and better healthcare.

“India Comes for Trust.”

We started with a focused portfolio and a commitment to building long-term relationships with doctors, healthcare professionals and patients.

From an idea to a growing business

We strengthened our product portfolio, established our presence in the market and began building a network of healthcare professionals.

Our focus remained on:

  • Quality pharmaceutical products
  • Strong doctor relationships
  • Ethical marketing
  • Reliable service
  • Long-term brand building

2024 was the year of foundation and learning.

2024

Building the Foundation

2025

Expanding Our Presence

From foundation to expansion

Ramedi Pharmaceuticals continued to expand its product portfolio and market presence.

We strengthened our presence across key therapeutic segments and focused on creating brands that address genuine healthcare needs.

2025 marked an important step forward in our journey - growing our reach while strengthening the trust we had built.

A stronger team. A wider portfolio. A bigger vision.

With an expanding field force and growing presence across Mumbai and Maharashtra, Ramedi Pharmaceuticals entered a new phase of growth.

Our focus is now on:

  • Expanding our doctor network
  • Strengthening our sales team
  • Building powerful pharmaceutical brands
  • Expanding into new markets
  • Developing consumer healthcare opportunities

We are building Ramedi for the long term - with trust at the centre of everything we do.

2026
