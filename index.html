Benkomedi CD3

MRP 227
Nutritional Supplement

Calcium Carbonate + Vitamin D3 + Methylcobalamin + L-Methylfolate + Pyridoxal-5-Phosphate

Supports bone health and corrects nutritional deficiencies with calcium, vitamin D3 and B-vitamins.

Key Ingredients

Calcium Carbonate (Elemental Calcium 500 mg) builds and maintains strong bones and teeth. Vitamin D3 (2000 IU) enhances calcium and phosphorus absorption. Methylcobalamin (1500 mcg) supports nerve function and red blood cell production. L-Methylfolate Calcium (1 mg) aids new blood cell formation. Pyridoxal-5-Phosphate (20 mg) acts as a coenzyme for metabolic and bone functions.

Uses & Benefits

Manages and prevents osteoporosis, osteomalacia and low calcium levels. Recommended during pregnancy, breastfeeding or post-surgical recovery. Helps correct vitamin B12 and folate gaps, reducing nerve pain and fatigue.

Dosage

Take orally, preferably after a meal to maximize absorption and lower the chance of stomach upset. Follow your doctor's exact instructions regarding dose and duration.

Magrache Q10

MRP 269
Nutraceutical Supplement

Magnesium + Coenzyme Q10 + Riboflavin

Supports cellular energy, bone structure and helps manage migraine headaches.

Key Ingredients

Each tablet contains Mineral (Magnesium Oxide, equivalent to Elemental Magnesium 340 mg), Coenzyme Q10 150 mg and Vitamin (Riboflavin) 1.6 mg, along with standard anticaking, dispersing, stabilizing and binding agents.

Key Benefits

Supports general health parameters and overall well-being. Helps maintain normal bone structure and healthy muscle function. Aids cellular energy production and provides antioxidant support. Supports metabolic health and energy conversion within cells.

Effects of Deficiency

Lack of magnesium can cause altered bone health, muscle weakness, general fatigue and lowered energy levels.

Gabbion NT 100

MRP 154
Prescription Medicine

Gabapentin + Nortriptyline

Combination medicine for neuropathic (nerve) pain, reducing abnormal nerve activity.

How It Works

Works by reducing abnormal nerve activity and blocking the transmission of pain signals, helping relieve neuropathic pain caused by nerve damage or injury.

Common Side Effects

Constipation, weight gain, dry mouth, difficulty in urination, nausea, dizziness or drowsiness. Consult your doctor if side effects persist.

Directions & Warnings

May be taken with or without food, at the same time every day. Avoid driving or alcohol if you feel dizzy or drowsy. Use with caution in liver/kidney problems. Not recommended for children below 18 unless prescribed. For Healthcare Professional Use only.

Gabbion NT 400

MRP 249
Prescription Medicine

Gabapentin 400 mg + Nortriptyline 10 mg

Higher-strength combination for neuropathic pain management under medical supervision.

How It Works

Reduces abnormal nerve activity and blocks transmission of pain signals, helping relieve neuropathic pain caused by nerve damage.

Common Side Effects

Constipation, weight gain, dry mouth, difficulty in urination, nausea, dizziness or drowsiness.

Directions & Warnings

Take at the same time daily, with or without food. Avoid alcohol and driving if drowsy. Consult your doctor if you have heart, liver or kidney problems. Not for children below 18 unless prescribed. Prescription Medicine — for Healthcare Professional Use.

Headmedi-N 250

MRP 104
Prescription Medicine

Naproxen 250 mg + Domperidone 10 mg

Treats migraine headaches by relieving pain and associated nausea.

How It Works

Blocks chemical substances in the body that cause pain, relieving migraine headache, and blocks a brain chemical that causes migraine-associated nausea.

Common Side Effects

Headache, stomach pain, dry mouth, diarrhoea. Consult your doctor if symptoms persist or worsen.

Directions & Warnings

Take with food or milk to prevent stomach upset. Avoid alcohol. Inform your doctor of heart, liver, kidney, clotting or bleeding disorders. For Healthcare Professional Use / Prescription Medicine.

Paxed CR 12.5

MRP 179
Prescription Medicine

Paroxetine Controlled-Release 12.5 mg

Antidepressant for depression, OCD, panic disorder, PTSD and anxiety disorders.

How It Works

Sugar-free, high-dose Vitamin D3 oral solution supporting bones, immunity and healthy growth.

Panprime DSR

MRP 147
Prescription Medicine

Pantoprazole 40 mg + Domperidone 30 mg

Manages acidity, heartburn, acid reflux and associated gastric discomfort.

How It Works

Pantoprazole blocks the proton pumps responsible for stomach acid production. Domperidone enhances gastrointestinal motility, helping with nausea, vomiting and bloating.

Common Side Effects

Headache, diarrhoea or constipation, stomach discomfort, dry mouth, dizziness.

Directions & Warnings

Take before food, at the same time daily; swallow whole with water. Inform your doctor about liver/kidney issues or heart rhythm problems. For Prescription Use Only.

Vivinerv

MRP 269
Nutritional Supplement

Methylcobalamin + Alpha Lipoic Acid + Benfotiamine + Chromium Picolinate + Folic Acid + Myo-Inositol + Selenium

Comprehensive nutritional support for nerve health, energy metabolism and antioxidant protection.

Key Ingredients

Methylcobalamin (1500 mcg) supports nerve function and red blood cell formation. Alpha Lipoic Acid (100 mg) is a potent antioxidant. Benfotiamine (150 mg) supports carbohydrate metabolism. Chromium Picolinate (200 mcg) supports glucose metabolism. Folic Acid (1.5 mg) supports DNA synthesis. Myo-Inositol (100 mg) supports cellular signalling. Selenium (55 mcg) provides antioxidant and immune support.

Key Benefits

Supports nerve health, provides comprehensive nutritional support, aids energy metabolism, antioxidant protection, metabolic health and red blood cell formation.

Acezero-P

MRP 95
Prescription Medicine

Aceclofenac + Paracetamol

Short-term relief for painful inflammatory musculoskeletal or dental conditions.

Uses

Inflammatory joint pain such as osteoarthritis, musculoskeletal pain including back and muscle pain, and short-term relief from dental or post-operative pain, when prescribed.

Side Effects

Nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, stomach upset, reduced appetite and dizziness — usually mild and manageable by taking the tablet with food.

Important Information

Not intended for routine everyday pain or uncomplicated fever. Tell your doctor about stomach, kidney, liver or heart conditions before use.

Medicitalo Forte

MRP 149
Prescription Medicine

Escitalopram 10 mg + Clonazepam 0.5 mg

Combination antidepressant and anxiolytic for anxiety disorders with depressive symptoms.

How It Works

Escitalopram increases availability of serotonin to improve mood and reduce anxiety/depression. Clonazepam enhances calming brain chemicals to reduce anxiety.

Common Side Effects

Drowsiness, dizziness, nausea, dry mouth, headache, fatigue, sleep changes, increased sweating.

Important Precautions

May cause drowsiness — avoid driving until effects are known. Avoid alcohol. Clonazepam may cause dependence with prolonged use; do not stop suddenly. Prescription Medicine — use only as directed by the physician.
